ARCHIVE: An HIV-Risk Mapper AI with Spatiotemporal GIS-Based Decision Support System for HIV Prevention in Region XII using MLR-LSTM, Hotspot Detection, Time Series Decomposition, and an LLM Chatbot
Key System Features
1. GIS-Based HIV Risk Mapping

Visualizes HIV cases, transmission patterns, and risk levels across Region XII through interactive GIS maps, heatmaps, and geospatial analytics, enabling users to identify high-risk municipalities and geographic trends.

2. AI-Powered Risk Prediction (MLR-LSTM)

Utilizes a Multiple Linear Regression–Long Short-Term Memory (MLR-LSTM) model to forecast future HIV incidence by analyzing historical, spatial, temporal, and demographic data, allowing health officials to anticipate potential outbreaks and prioritize interventions.

3. Hotspot Detection

Automatically identifies emerging, persistent, and declining HIV hotspots using spatial statistical techniques to support targeted public health interventions and efficient resource allocation.

4. Time Series Decomposition

Analyzes HIV surveillance data by separating long-term trends, seasonal variations, and irregular patterns, providing deeper epidemiological insights and improving forecasting accuracy.

5. AI-Assisted Decision Support Dashboard

Provides an integrated dashboard displaying real-time analytics, municipality risk rankings, hotspot summaries, forecasting results, key performance indicators, and AI-generated intervention recommendations to assist healthcare professionals in evidence-based decision-making.

6. HIV Surveillance and Monitoring

Tracks HIV cases across Region XII by municipality, province, age group, sex, and reporting period through interactive dashboards, charts, and geospatial visualizations, enabling continuous monitoring of disease trends.

7. LLM-Powered Health Assistant

Provides AI-assisted responses to HIV-related inquiries, delivers reliable educational information, explains HIV prevention strategies, interprets surveillance results, guides users to appropriate healthcare services, and assists healthcare professionals with data interpretation and decision support.

8. Automated Alerts and Notifications

Automatically generates alerts for increasing HIV cases, newly detected hotspots, abnormal forecasting results, and other significant surveillance events, enabling timely responses from healthcare authorities.

9. Report Generation and Data Management

Generates surveillance reports, forecasting summaries, hotspot analyses, and executive reports while securely managing HIV surveillance records and supporting data import, export, and reporting.

10. User and Security Management

Implements role-based access control, secure authentication, audit logging, encryption, and compliance with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 to ensure the confidentiality, integrity, and security of sensitive information.

11. Health Facility and Resource Mapping

Maps HIV treatment hubs, testing centers, hospitals, laboratories, rural health units, and community support organizations, helping users locate healthcare services while assisting health authorities in strategic resource planning.

12. AI Risk Assessment Tool

Allows users to anonymously complete an HIV risk assessment questionnaire that evaluates potential risk factors and provides personalized prevention recommendations while encouraging consultation with qualified healthcare professionals when appropriate.

13. Intelligent Referral System

Recommends the nearest HIV testing centers, treatment hubs, healthcare facilities, or support organizations based on the user's location and healthcare needs, helping individuals quickly access appropriate HIV-related services.

14. Community Engagement Portal

Provides a secure public portal where individuals, including people living with HIV (PLHIV), can anonymously access educational resources, announcements, frequently asked questions, downloadable materials, community support information, and submit feedback to healthcare authorities.

15. AI Intervention Prioritization

Uses AI-generated forecasting results, hotspot analysis, healthcare resource availability, and risk indicators to prioritize municipalities or barangays that require immediate testing, awareness campaigns, treatment expansion, or public health interventions.

16. Explainable AI Reports

Improves transparency by explaining the key variables and contributing factors behind AI predictions, municipal risk classifications, hotspot identification, and intervention recommendations, increasing confidence in AI-assisted decision-making.

17. Executive Decision Dashboard

Provides healthcare executives and decision-makers with a centralized overview of current HIV hotspots, forecasted HIV cases, intervention priorities, healthcare resource availability, surveillance performance indicators, and AI-generated strategic recommendations for public health planning.
