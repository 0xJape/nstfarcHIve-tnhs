# Project Overview

## ARCHIVE: An HIV-Risk Mapper AI with Spatiotemporal GIS-Based Decision Support System for HIV Prevention in Region XII using MLR-LSTM, Hotspot Detection, Time Series Decomposition, and an LLM Chatbot

ARCHIVE is an AI-assisted Spatiotemporal Geographic Information System (GIS)-Based Decision Support System designed to strengthen HIV surveillance, forecasting, prevention, and public health planning across Region XII. The system integrates Multiple Linear Regression–Long Short-Term Memory (MLR-LSTM), hotspot detection, time series decomposition, Geographic Information Systems (GIS), and Large Language Model (LLM) technologies to transform HIV surveillance data into meaningful insights that support evidence-based public health decisions.

The system is designed to serve two primary user groups. The first consists of healthcare professionals, epidemiologists, and government agencies who require advanced surveillance, forecasting, and decision-support tools to monitor HIV transmission, identify emerging hotspots, allocate healthcare resources efficiently, and develop timely intervention strategies. Through interactive dashboards, AI-generated recommendations, predictive analytics, and geospatial visualizations, ARCHIVE enables health authorities to proactively respond to changing HIV trends and improve strategic planning throughout Region XII.

The second user group includes members of the public, particularly individuals seeking reliable HIV-related information and people living with HIV (PLHIV). Through a secure public portal, users may anonymously access educational resources, prevention materials, HIV awareness content, AI-assisted guidance, and directories of nearby HIV testing centers, treatment hubs, healthcare facilities, and community support organizations. The platform also includes an anonymous AI-powered HIV risk assessment tool that helps individuals better understand their potential risk factors while encouraging consultation with qualified healthcare professionals when appropriate.

To further improve access to healthcare services, ARCHIVE incorporates an Intelligent Referral System that recommends appropriate HIV testing centers, treatment hubs, and healthcare facilities based on a user's location and healthcare needs. Authorized healthcare administrators may also interact with users by answering inquiries, providing verified HIV information, recommending appropriate services, and directing individuals to available healthcare programs while maintaining user privacy and confidentiality.

The platform combines advanced AI technologies with interactive GIS mapping to visualize HIV cases, identify transmission hotspots, forecast future HIV incidence, prioritize intervention areas, and explain the reasoning behind AI-generated predictions through Explainable AI. Automated alerts, executive dashboards, comprehensive reporting tools, and secure role-based access control further enhance the system's ability to support healthcare professionals in making informed, data-driven decisions.

ARCHIVE is not intended to diagnose HIV or replace professional medical consultation. Instead, it serves as an intelligent decision support and public health information platform that empowers healthcare authorities with advanced surveillance and forecasting capabilities while providing individuals with accessible education, healthcare navigation, and trustworthy AI-assisted guidance. By bridging advanced epidemiological analytics with community engagement and public health education, ARCHIVE contributes to strengthening HIV prevention, improving access to healthcare services, supporting evidence-based interventions, and enhancing HIV response efforts throughout Region XII.
