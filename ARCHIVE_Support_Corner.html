Need urgent emotional support?

The National Center for Mental Health crisis hotline is reported as available 24/7. For immediate danger or a life-threatening emergency, contact emergency services. You are welcome to seek help without explaining your HIV status to ARCHIVE.

Common questions

Do I need to share my HIV status to browse these links?

No. You can browse this ARCHIVE page without providing your HIV status or creating an account. External organizations may have their own requirements when you contact them directly.

Will ARCHIVE book my counseling or treatment appointment?

No. The resources here open official external sites. Keep using ARCHIVE's existing appointment-booking feature for its supported services. Each outside provider has its own contact or appointment process.

What if I feel judged or my privacy is violated?

Philippine Republic Act No. 11166 protects HIV-related confidentiality and prohibits forms of HIV-related discrimination. Read the law in the resources above and consider seeking advice from a qualified advocate or legal professional about your specific situation.

Are these support links guaranteed to be available today?

No. ARCHIVE links to source pages that were reviewed during prototype development; availability, locations, eligibility and contact details may change. Check directly with the organization before traveling or relying on a service.
